Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This policy explains what personal data Otiumate collects through otiumate.com, why we collect it, and what choices you have. We keep it short and specific — it describes what this website actually does, not what a template says.

Who we are

Otiumate is an AI automation and products studio based in Egypt. For any privacy question, or to exercise the rights described below, email otiumate@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Name, email address, and the message you write Only when you submit the contact form To read and reply to your enquiry
Usage data — pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser, referring site, and interactions such as clicking a call-to-action or opening a project On every visit, via Google Analytics To understand which parts of the site are useful and improve them
IP address and standard server logs On every visit, by our hosting and CDN providers To deliver the site and protect it from abuse

We do not ask for payment details, we do not require an account, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies to distinguish repeat visits and measure how the site is used. These are analytics cookies — we do not run advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal data.

You can block these at any time using your browser's cookie settings, a content blocker, or Google's opt-out browser add-on. Blocking them does not affect how the site works.

Who else receives your data

We use a small number of third-party services. Each receives only what it needs to do its job:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions and forwards them to us by email.
  • Google Analytics — usage measurement, as described above.
  • Vercel and Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery. They process IP addresses in server logs.
  • Google Fonts, Fontshare, and Simple Icons — serve fonts and icons. Because these load from their own servers, your browser's IP address is visible to them when a page loads.

These providers operate outside Egypt, so using this site involves international transfer of the data above. We do not share your data with anyone else, and we never sell it.

How long we keep it

Contact form messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any follow-up work, and are deleted on request. Google Analytics data is retained according to the retention period configured in our Analytics property.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, have it deleted, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email otiumate@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and sends standard security headers. No website can promise perfect security, but we limit what we collect, which is the most effective protection available.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Questions? Email otiumate@gmail.com — we read everything that comes in.
